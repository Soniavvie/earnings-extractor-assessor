Evofem Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update
SAN DIEGO, CA, November 13, 2026 — Evofem Biosciences, Inc. (Evofem or the Company) (OTCID: EVFM), today announced financial results for the three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026.
Saundra Pelletier, CEO of Evofem, said, “While third quarter net sales reflect the cumulative impact of operating without dedicated growth capital for brand promotion and physician outreach since late 2022, we are proud of the discipline, efficiency and tenacity demonstrated across the organization. Through rigorous expense management, we generated $0.7 million of operating income during the quarter while continuing to support patients and healthcare providers who rely on our innovative sexual health products. We remain confident in the long-term value of PHEXX and SOLOSEC and believe targeted investment in commercial activities would provide meaningful opportunities to accelerate growth.”
Highlights since July 1, 2026, include:
● | Income from operations was $0.7 million for Q3 2026, compared to $1.0 million for Q3 2025.
● | Net income attributable to common stockholders was $0.9 million, or $0.01 per basic and ($0.00) per diluted share, for Q3 2026, compared to a net loss of $1.7 million, or ($0.01) per basic and diluted share, for Q3 2025.
● | Contracted with a second pharmaceutical manufacturer to produce PHEXX® (lactic acid, citric acid and potassium bitartrate), an important milestone in its strategy to support long-term growth, enhance manufacturing flexibility, and reduce costs.
● | Shipped the 500,000th box of PHEXX in July 2026, representing hormone-free, on-demand contraception for six million acts of sex.
● | A fifth study site was added in the ongoing investigator-led, NIH-funded Phase 4 clinical trial evaluating the effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of SOLOSEC® (secnidazole) 2g oral granules (single-dose, one time) versus metronidazole (twice daily for seven days) for the treatment of Trichomonas vaginalis in men and women. Study investigators hypothesize that the rate of repeat infections with T. vaginalis will be 1.75 lower in the SOLOSEC group versus the multi-dose oral metronidazole arm and that single-dose SOLOSEC will have higher initial cost but will be more cost effective compared to multi-dose metronidazole, largely due to lower breakthrough rates of infection.1
● | Regulatory work is underway for SOLOSEC in Uganda, the first planned market in Sub-Saharan Africa under our April 2026 distribution agreement with Clovis Davis Pharmaceuticals.

Third Quarter Financial Results
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net sales were $4.6 million, versus $5.0 million in the prior year period. The decrease was primarily due to lower ex-factory sales for PHEXX in the 2026 period. Wholesalers have continued to sell through inventory purchased in December 2025, ahead of the January 2026 price increase. Additional factors include the ongoing absence of funding for marketing and a reduction in filled sales territories in the current quarter. This was partially offset by an increase in SOLOSEC units sold, the wholesale average cost (WAC) increase that took effect on January 1, 2026, and improved gross-to-net realization for both products in the 2026 period.
Total operating expenses were $3.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, versus $4.0 million in the prior year period. The decrease was driven by cost containment measures across all aspects of the business.
Income from operations was $0.7 million, compared to $1.0 million in the prior year quarter.
Net income attributable to common stockholders was $0.9 million, or $0.01 per basic and ($0.00) per diluted share, in the current period versus a net loss attributable to common stockholders of $1.7 million, or $(0.01) per basic and diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.
Liquidity
Restricted cash and cash equivalents was $0.3 million as of September 30, 2026, versus $0.6 million as of December 31, 2025.
About Evofem
Evofem Biosciences is a San Diego-based pharmaceutical company commercializing two FDA-approved sexual and reproductive health products:
● | PHEXX® (lactic acid, citric acid, and potassium bitartrate) - the first and only hormone-free, on-demand prescription contraceptive vaginal gel. Visit phexx.com to learn more and for important safety information.
● | SOLOSEC® (secnidazole) 2g oral granules - an FDA-approved oral antibiotic for the treatment of two sexual health diseases: bacterial vaginosis (BV), a common vaginal infection, in females 12 years of age and older, and trichomoniasis, a common sexually transmitted infection (STI), in people 12 years of age and older. SOLOSEC provides a complete course of therapy in just one dose. Visit solosec.com to learn more and for important safety information.
PHEXX® and SOLOSEC® are registered trademarks of Evofem Biosciences, Inc.
