Medifast Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
October 26, 2026
BALTIMORE – (BUSINESS WIRE) — Medifast (NYSE: MED), the metabolic health and wellness company known for its science-backed comprehensive metabolic health system, Trilivy™, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026
•Revenue: $72.0 million, with revenue per active earning coach of $7,064
•Independent active earning coaches of 10,200
•Net loss of $17.5 million (non-GAAP adjusted net loss of $2.1 million)
•Loss per diluted share ("EPS") of $1.57 (non-GAAP adjusted loss per diluted share of $0.19)
•Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Investment Securities of $172.5 million with no debt

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Third quarter 2026 revenue decreased 19.4% to $72.0 million from $89.4 million for the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by a decrease in the number of active earning coaches. The total number of active earning coaches decreased 47.7% to 10,200 compared to 19,500 for the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by continued pressure with client acquisition reflecting broader challenges in the operating environment, including rapid adoption of GLP-1 medications for weight loss. While the company is actively executing on metabolic health, it expects the number of active earning coaches to continue to decline through the end of 2026. The average revenue per active earning coach was $7,064, compared to $4,585 for the third quarter last year, an
1
increase of 54.1% which was driven by the decrease in the number of active earning coaches, facilitated in part by our focus on efficient and more productive coach network structures.
During the second quarter of 2026, the company began execution on a cost savings project (“Catalyst”) with the majority of its execution taking place this quarter. One of the key initiatives under Catalyst was a restructuring action (“Catalyst Restructuring”) intended to simplify the business and reduce costs by reducing the size and reassessing the geographic locations of the company’s distribution network, streamlining the company’s internal use software, and aligning organization resources with current revenue levels.
Gross profit decreased 19.5% to $50.0 million from $62.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in gross profit was due to lower sales volumes and Catalyst Restructuring charges, partially offset by costs to reformulate the Essential product line in the prior comparable period. The company's gross profit margin was 69.5% for both the current and prior year comparable period, primarily due to 230 basis points attributable to Catalyst Restructuring charges, substantially offset by 220 basis points of costs to reformulate the Essential line in the prior period. Non-GAAP adjusted gross profit, which excludes expenses in connection with the Catalyst Restructuring, decreased 16.9% to $51.7 million and non-GAAP adjusted gross profit margin increased 220 basis points year-over-year to 71.7%.
Selling, general, and administrative expenses (“SG&A”) increased 4.8% to $69.4 million compared to $66.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in SG&A was primarily due to a $13.8 million increase for Catalyst Restructuring charges, partially offset by a $6.6 million decrease in coach compensation on lower volume and fewer active earning coaches and a $1.4 million decrease in IT consulting spend. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A increased 22 percentage points year-over-year to 96.3% of revenue, as compared to 74.1% for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in SG&A as a percentage of revenue was primarily due to approximately 19 percentage points of Catalyst Restructuring costs, and 3 percentage points related to our Trilivy Convention for which no convention was held in the comparable year-ago period. Non-GAAP adjusted SG&A, which excludes expenses related to Catalyst Restructuring, decreased 16.0% to $55.6 million and non-GAAP adjusted SG&A as a percentage of revenue increased 310 basis points year-over-year to 77.2%.
The company's loss from operations for the period was $19.4 million compared to $4.1 million in the prior year comparable period. As a percentage of revenue, loss from operations was 26.9% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 4.6% in the prior-year comparable period due to the factors described above impacting gross profit and SG&A expenses. Non-GAAP adjusted loss from operations improved by 3.2% to $4.0 million. Non-GAAP adjusted loss from operations as a percentage of revenue was 5.5%, a change of 90 basis points from 4.6% in the year-ago period.
Other income increased $0.1 million to $1.5 million compared to $1.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Other income is primarily comprised of interest income on cash, cash equivalents, and investments in both periods.
Income tax benefit for the period was $0.3 million, an effective rate of 1.8%, as compared to $0.4 million for the third quarter of 2025, an effective rate of 14.9%. Due to the existence of a full valuation allowance against its deferred tax assets recorded as of December 31, 2025, the company calculated income tax expense for the current period based on actual results for the quarter. The company’s income tax provision for the period reflects discrete items, primarily a $0.4 million change in unrecognized tax benefits due to finalization of a state tax examination, partially offset by $0.1 million of state income taxes. Due to the existence of a full valuation allowance against deferred tax assets, the non-GAAP adjustments do not impact the income tax benefit for the period.

In the third quarter of 2026, the company's net loss was $17.5 million, or $1.57 per share, based on approximately 11.2 million shares of common stock outstanding compared to $2.3 million, or $0.21 per share, based on approximately 11.0 million shares of common stock outstanding in the prior year comparable period. Non-GAAP adjusted net loss was $2.1 million, or $0.19 per diluted share.
In the third quarter of 2026, the company's adjusted EBITDA, which excludes stock-based compensation and Catalyst charges, was $0.4 million, compared to $0.3 million in the prior year comparable period. The company's adjusted EBITDA excluding interest income was $1.9 million, compared to $1.7 million in the prior year comparable period.
Capital Allocation and Balance Sheet
The company’s balance sheet remains strong with cash, cash equivalents and investment securities of $172.5 million and no debt as of September 30, 2026, compared to $167.3 million in cash, cash equivalents and investment securities and no debt at December 31, 2025.
Outlook
The company expects fourth quarter 2026 revenue to be in the range of $55 million to $75 million and fourth quarter 2026 income per share to be in the range of $0.00 to $0.25. The company expects full year 2026 revenue to be in the range of $280 million to $300 million and full year 2026 loss per share to range from $1.80 to $2.05. For the full year 2026, on a non-GAAP basis, we expect loss per share between $0.45 and $0.70. We are expecting fourth quarter Adjusted EBITDA excluding interest income to range from $2 million to $6 million. For the full year 2026, we are expecting Adjusted EBITDA excluding interest income to range from $10 million to $14 million.

About Medifast®:
Medifast (NYSE: MED) is the metabolic health and wellness company known for its science-backed comprehensive metabolic health system, Trilivy™. Designed to help address the challenges of metabolic dysfunction, the company’s holistic approach integrates science-backed plans and products, personal 1:1 coaching, a supportive community, and behavioral science support to develop healthy habits.
Driven to improve metabolic health through advanced science and comprehensive behavioral support, Medifast has introduced Metabolic Synchronization®, a breakthrough science that targets metabolic dysfunction through a comprehensive system focused on fat loss, lean mass preservation, and long-term health. Trilivy's comprehensive three-part metabolic health system is designed to help people reset their metabolism, refine their health, and renew their lives. By integrating science, coaching, and healthy habits into a single approach, Trilivy helps people look, feel, and live better.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In an effort to provide investors with additional information regarding our results as determined by Generally Accepted Accounting Principles ("GAAP"), we disclose various non-GAAP financial measures in this quarterly earnings press release, our quarterly report on Form 10-Q, and other public disclosures. The following GAAP financial measures have been presented on an as-adjusted basis: cost of sales, selling, general and administrative expenses, income (loss) from operations, net income (loss), and diluted earnings (loss) per share. As-adjusted cost of sales, selling general and administrative expenses, income (loss) from operations, net income (loss), and diluted earnings (loss) per share exclude the impact of certain amounts related to our Catalyst Restructuring efforts and have not been calculated in accordance with GAAP. We disclose Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, as a performance measure which excludes recurring items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance: interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, and stock-based compensation expense, as well as charges related to Catalyst Restructuring in the current period. We disclose Adjusted EBITDA excluding interest income, a non-GAAP measure, as a performance measure which includes the previously noted adjustments to arrive at Adjusted EBITDA with the exception of interest income, as our current cash, cash equivalents and investment securities balance makes interest income a notable performance data point. A reconciliation of each of these non-GAAP financial measures to its most comparable GAAP financial measure is included below. These non-GAAP financial measures are not intended to replace GAAP financial measures.
We use these non-GAAP financial measures internally to evaluate and manage the company’s operations because we believe they provide useful supplemental information regarding the company’s on-going economic performance. We have chosen to provide this information to investors to enable them to perform more meaningful comparisons of operating results and as a means to emphasize the results of on-going operations.
