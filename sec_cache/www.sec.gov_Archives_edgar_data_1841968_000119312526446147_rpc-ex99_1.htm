Ridgepost Capital Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

DALLAS, Nov. 04, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Ridgepost Capital, Inc. (NYSE: RPC) (“Ridgepost Capital”), a leading private markets solutions provider, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

A presentation of the quarterly financials is available at https://ir.ridgepostcapital.com/quarterly-results.

“Ridgepost Capital delivered strong and consistent third-quarter results, as fee-paying AUM increased 21% year-over-year to more than $35 billion while margins through the first nine months of 2026 were stable year-over-year,” said Luke Sarsfield, Ridgepost Capital Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our results demonstrate the benefits of the One Ridgepost platform. We raised and deployed $1.3 billion of fee-paying AUM in the third quarter, bringing the year-to-date total to a new record of nearly $4.5 billion. Notably, since June 2024, cross-selling has contributed approximately $1.7 billion to firmwide fundraising, reflecting increased collaboration across our strategies. While the operating environment remains fluid, the contractual nature of our model, our complementary platform, and differentiated investment performance continue to drive consistency and strength in our results.”

Stock Repurchase Program

During the third quarter, we repurchased approximately 1.78 million shares of our common stock at an average price of $8.36 per share, totaling approximately $15 million. On November 3, 2026, our Board of Directors approved a $30 million increase of the amount authorized under our share repurchase program, resulting in approximately $30 million of available repurchase authorization.

Declaration of Dividend

Our Board of Directors has declared a cash dividend of $0.04 per share of Class A and Class B common stock, payable on December 18, 2026, to stockholders of record as of November 30, 2026.

About Ridgepost Capital

Ridgepost Capital (NYSE: RPC) is a leading private markets solutions provider with over $50 billion in assets under management as of September 30, 2026. Ridgepost Capital invests across Private Equity, Private Credit, and Venture Capital in access-constrained strategies, with a focus on the middle and lower-middle market. Ridgepost Capital’s products have a global investor base and aim to deliver compelling risk-adjusted returns. For additional information, please visit www.ridgepostcapital.com.

Key Financial & Operating Metrics

FPAUM reflects the assets from which we earn management and advisory fees. Our vehicles typically earn management and advisory fees based on committed capital, and in certain cases, net invested capital, depending on the fee terms. Management and advisory fees based on committed capital are not affected by market appreciation or depreciation.

 

AUM reflects the assets that we manage, and is calculated as the sum of: (i) net asset value (“NAV”) of our clients’ and funds’ underlying investments as of the most recently available date; (ii) drawn and undrawn debt (excluding capital call lines); (iii) uncalled capital commitments (net

 

 

of deferred purchase price and not in excess of total capital commitments, as applicable) as of the NAV record date; (iv) incremental commitments raised since NAV record date. In situations where NAV data is not available, such as with certain advisory relationships, we use FPAUM.
