Retension Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

●IPO completed in October 2026 to support the continued development of RTN-001 for uncontrolled and resistant hypertension, affecting ~30 million Americans
●Upsized IPO generated $51.75 million in gross proceeds
●Topline data for Phase 2b clinical trial expected in first half of 2027

FALLS CHURCH, Va., November 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Retension Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: RTSN) (“Retension” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing medicines for the treatment of hypertension and other cardiovascular diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

“Completing our initial public offering was an important step for Retension, and it provides the capital to complete our Phase 2b clinical trial of RTN-001 and prepare for Phase 3,” said Eric Keller, Chief Executive Officer of Retension Pharmaceuticals. “This year we have also built a leadership team with deep experience advancing cardiovascular and metabolic therapies from clinical development through approval, and formed a Scientific Advisory Board of leading investigators in hypertension, cardiology and nephrology. We remain focused on executing the Phase 2b trial, with initial data expected in the first half of 2027, as we work to bring a new treatment option to patients with uncontrolled and resistant hypertension.”

Recent Corporate Highlights and Upcoming Milestones

Completed initial public offering: In October 2026, Retension completed its upsized initial public offering of 4,312,500 shares of common stock at $12.00 per share, which includes the full exercise of the underwriters’ option to purchase 562,500 additional shares. The offering generated gross proceeds of $51.75 million and net proceeds of approximately $45.1 million. Retension’s common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the ticker symbol “RTSN” on October 9, 2026.

Phase 2b clinical trial of RTN-001 ongoing: The randomized, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging trial is evaluating RTN-001 in approximately 280 patients with uncontrolled hypertension. The study's primary endpoint is the change from baseline in office-seated systolic blood pressure at Week 4 versus placebo. The trial will also inform dose selection for Phase 3. Topline data are expected in the first half of 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

●Cash position: Cash and cash equivalents were $9.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $18.9 million as of December 31, 2025. These amounts do not reflect the net proceeds of the IPO, which closed after quarter-end. Including net IPO proceeds of approximately $45.1 million, pro forma cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 were approximately $54.3 million.
●Research and development expenses: Research and development expenses were $5.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to Phase 2b clinical trial costs.
●General and administrative expenses: General and administrative expenses were $1.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $0.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher personnel costs.
●Net loss: Net loss was $5.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss of $1.5 million for the third quarter of 2025 due to additional R&D and G&A expenses.

About Retension Pharmaceuticals

Retension Pharmaceuticals is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing medicines for the treatment of hypertension and other cardiovascular diseases. The Company’s lead clinical candidate, RTN-001, is in development for patients with uncontrolled and resistant hypertension.

About RTN-001

Retension Pharmaceuticals is developing RTN-001, a cardiovascular-targeted, next-generation PDE5 inhibitor designed to directly enhance nitric oxide signaling in central arteries not adequately reached by first-generation PDE5 inhibitors, thereby addressing a core driver of hypertension. RTN-001 has been studied in multiple clinical trials totaling 265 subjects. RTN-001 has a demonstrated efficacy and safety profile in patients and healthy volunteers with clinically meaningful reductions in blood pressure. A Phase 2b trial of RTN-001 in hypertension is ongoing. RTN-001 is being developed under an exclusive world-wide license from Sanofi S.A.
