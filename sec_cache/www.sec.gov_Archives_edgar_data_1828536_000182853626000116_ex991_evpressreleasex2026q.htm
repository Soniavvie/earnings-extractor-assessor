Energy Vault Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results, Affirms Recently Raised Full-Year 2026 Revenue Guidance and Provides Preliminary 2027 Financial Guidance

Contract backlog reached $2.3 billion, up 15% sequentially and 147% year-over-year
Q3 2026 revenue of $13.1 million in line with consensus estimates
GAAP gross margin of 71.0% and adjusted gross margin of 81.1%. Excluding the benefit of tariff refunds, gross margins were 37.9% and 48.0%, respectively
Total cash increased to $236 million, up 59% sequentially and 281% year-over-year
Completed second portfolio acquisition of 2026 from Goshe Energy Storage (2.3GW, including 350 MW ready to build); 100% of cash purchase price paid in Q3 2026
Received formal grid interconnection approval for 125MW/1GWh Stoney Creek project in Australia
Multiple project financings in U.S. and Australia underway
Affirming full-year 2026 revenue of $290–310 million, at the top half of the previous guidance range with expected Q4 project deliveries supporting guidance
Preliminary full-year 2027 revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance range of $600-700 million and $40-50 million, respectively
WESTLAKE VILLAGE, Calif., November 10, 2026 – Energy Vault Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: NRGV) (“Energy Vault” or “the Company”), a global energy infrastructure company supporting grid reliability and next-generation AI and high-performance computing infrastructure, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and guidance updates.
“During the third quarter, we continued advancing our integrated Build, Own & Operate strategy, expanding our global development pipeline and strengthening our cash and overall financial position,” said Robert Piconi, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our backlog reached $2.3 billion as of November 10th, third quarter total cash increased almost 3x year-over-year, and we achieved important financing, acquisition and development milestones that support the long-term growth of our owned infrastructure platform.”
“In AI infrastructure, we secured non-recourse financing for 275 MW of Rolls-Royce MTU reciprocating engine generation capacity, advanced our Digital Vault Sulcis development in Italy following its designation as a project of pre-eminent national strategic interest, and started construction of the Snyder AI modular data center project. During the fourth quarter, we began deliveries to the recently announced 1.25GW hybrid generation and energy storage project supporting AI compute infrastructure in Texas.
In our Asset Vault portfolio, we completed our second acquisition this year following the previously announced Japanese portfolio acquisition from Baywa Renewables in Q2, acquiring 2.3 GW of U.S. BESS development projects from Goshe Energy Storage, including 350 MW ready to build. In Australia, we achieved important development milestones at our 125 MW / 1 GWh Stoney Creek BESS in Australia, including formal grid interconnection approval last week, which paves the way for an accelerated construction ramp up. Multiple project financings in U.S. and Australia are underway.

Our third quarter financial performance reflects our previously discussed timing of deliveries and continued strengthening of our balance sheet and liquidity. We remain focused on efficient project execution for our customers, capital discipline and progressing our development pipeline towards operating assets and long term, recurring EBITDA.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Backlog reached $2.3 billion as of November 10, 2026, up approximately $1.4 billion, or 147%, year-over-year, comprising approximately $800 million of Build and Transfer backlog and $1.5 billion of Build, Own & Operate backlog
•Q3 2026 revenue of $13.1 million decreased $20.3 million, or 61%, from the prior-year period in-line with expectations based upon planned project deliveries
•Q3 2026 GAAP gross profit was approximately $9.3 million, compared with approximately $9.0 million in the prior-year period, driven in part by tariff refunds of $4.3 million. In the quarter, GAAP gross margin was 71.0%, compared to the prior-year period of 27.0%. Excluding the benefit of tariff refunds, GAAP gross margin was 37.9%
•Q3 2026 adjusted gross profit (which adds back non-cash D&A to gross profit) was approximately $10.6 million and adjusted gross margin was 81.1%, compared to the prior-year period of 30.6%. Excluding the benefit of tariff refunds, adjusted gross margin would have been 48.0%
•Q3 2026 operating expenses were $33.9 million, an increase of 27% year-over-year, Q3 2026 adjusted operating expenses were $25.2 million, an increase of $9.0 million, or 56%, year-over-year
•Q3 2026 GAAP net loss was approximately $28.2 million, compared with approximately $26.8 million in the prior-year period. GAAP net loss per share was approximately $(0.17)
•Q3 2026 adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $14.7 million, compared with an approximately $6.0 million loss in the prior-year period
•Q3 2026 adjusted net loss was approximately $22.9 million and adjusted net loss per share was $(0.14).
•Total cash and cash equivalents, including restricted cash, was $236 million as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $88 million sequentially and $174 million year-over-year. Corporate level recourse debt declined $12 million sequentially quarter-over-quarter

About Energy Vault
Energy Vault® is an integrated power infrastructure platform that builds, owns and operates flexible, reliable energy systems to accelerate time-to-power for utilities, independent power producers, industrial customers and the AI and data center market. At the core of its platform is a technology-agnostic, software-enabled architecture that is designed to accelerate project delivery, optimize performance and drive faster time-to-revenue. Energy Vault’s integrated solutions combine energy storage, generation and advanced energy management to deliver scalable infrastructure tailored to customer needs. Its portfolio spans short-, long- and multi-day duration storage, designed to enable reliability, flexibility and cost efficiency across applications. For utilities and grid operators, Energy Vault provides firm, flexible capacity that enhances grid stability and helps to ensure reliable power delivery. For industrial and data center customers, the platform enables resilient, cost-efficient power supply to support critical operations. Through its Build, Own & Operate model, Energy Vault generates long-term, recurring revenues while delivering project execution excellence across development, delivery and operations. By combining innovation with disciplined execution, Energy Vault is redefining how power infrastructure is developed and deployed – delivering reliability, flexibility and scale in a rapidly evolving global energy market. Please visit www.energyvault.com for additional information.

Bookings
Contracted bookings are from binding, fully executed customer agreements signed during the period or purchase orders received from customers during the period. Contingent option bookings are from projects where the Company holds an enforceable exclusive purchase right and intends to exercise that right, even if the option has not been exercised as of period end.
Bookings include agreements and purchase orders for third-party projects and services, IP licensing, and leasing, tolling, energy supply and offtake arrangements associated with energy infrastructure owned or operated by the Company or its affiliates. Aggregate contract value includes fixed contractual payments and estimated future variable payments over the applicable contractual term that the Company believes are probable of being realized.
For variable payments dependent on energy market conditions, the Company uses forecasts prepared by an independent third-party firm using simulation software that considers current and projected energy market dynamics, historical and forecasted volatility, and location-specific data. The Company considers the low-end simulation results to be probable. For variable payments dependent on customer consumption or utilization, the Company uses applicable contractual pricing and management’s estimates of expected consumption or utilization, considering customer-provided forecasts, anticipated operating ramp-up, available operating data and other relevant information, as applicable. Management evaluates these assumptions for reasonableness and includes only amounts it believes are probable of being realized.
Potential future IP royalties, uncontracted residual asset values, and amounts attributable to unexercised renewal or extension options are excluded from bookings. Due to the long-term nature of our contracts, bookings provide useful information to investors and management in evaluating the growth of our business and estimated future revenue associated with our customer arrangements.
Backlog
Backlog represents estimated future revenue or other income not yet recognized from (i) third-party projects and services, (ii) IP licensing agreements, and (iii) leasing, tolling, energy supply and offtake arrangements associated with energy infrastructure owned or operated by the Company or its affiliates, in each case associated with contracted bookings or contingent option bookings, as defined above.
Backlog includes contracted backlog and contingent option backlog. Contracted backlog reflects amounts associated with binding, fully executed agreements, inclusive of SOSA, which is expected to reach financial close imminently. Contingent option backlog reflects amounts associated with projects where the Company holds an enforceable exclusive purchase right and intends to exercise that right, even if the option has not been exercised as of period end, and is contingent on the Company exercising the applicable purchase right and subsequent project execution. If the Company does not exercise an option, or if the underlying terms or assumptions change such that inclusion is no longer appropriate, the related contingent option backlog is removed or updated in the period of change.
Backlog includes fixed contractual payments and estimated future variable payments over the remaining applicable contractual term that the Company believes are probable of being realized. For variable payments dependent on energy market conditions, the Company uses forecasts prepared by an independent third-party firm using simulation software that considers current and projected energy market dynamics, historical and forecasted volatility, and location-specific data. The Company considers the low-end simulation results to be probable. Our adjusted EBITDA measure has limitations as an analytical tool, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are:
•it does not reflect our cash expenditures, future requirements for capital expenditures, or contractual commitments;
